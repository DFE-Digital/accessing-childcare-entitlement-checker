This page lists the rules for the five childcare entitlement schemes. The rules engine uses these rules to evaluate eligibility.

All rules use the data from the application form.

## Fifteen hours for disadvantaged children

The system evaluates eligibility for the "fifteen hours for disadvantaged children" scheme.

### Summary of criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | England |
| **Age** | Exactly 2 years old |
| **Benefit eligibility** | Automatic or household benefits |

### Specific rules

The child must live in England. The child must be 2 years old.

The child can get eligibility in two ways:

* **Automatic eligibility**:
  * The child receives Disability Living Allowance.
  * OR the child has an Education, Health, and Care plan.
* **Benefit-based eligibility**:
  * The household has access to public funds.
  * AND the household receives Universal Credit.
  * OR the user or partner receives the Guaranteed Element of Pension Credit.

## Fifteen hours universal

The system evaluates eligibility for the "fifteen hours universal" scheme.

### Summary of criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | England |
| **Age** | Exactly 3 or 4 years old |
| **Income limits** | No limits |

### Specific rules

* The child must live in England.
* The child must be 3 or 4 years old.
* The scheme has no income limit.
* The scheme has no work limit.

## Tax-free childcare

The system evaluates eligibility for the "tax-free childcare" scheme.

### Summary of criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Age** | Up to 11 years old (or up to 16 years old if the child has a disability) |
| **Income limit** | Each parent must earn under £100,000 adjusted net income |
| **Exclusions** | Household cannot receive Universal Credit, childcare vouchers, or childcare grants |

### Specific rules

* **Age limit**:
  * The child must be 11 years old or younger.
  * The age limit is 16 years old or younger if the child receives Disability Living Allowance or has an Education, Health, and Care plan.
* **Exclusions**:
  * The household must not receive Universal Credit.
  * Parents must not receive childcare vouchers or childcare bursaries/grants.
* **Income limit**:
  * No parent can earn more than £100,000 adjusted net income.
* **Work requirements**:
  * Single parents:
    * The parent must work and earn above the minimum threshold.
    * OR the parent must be self-employed for less than 12 months.
    * OR the parent must be on sick leave or parental leave.
  * Couples:
    * Both parents must meet the work and income requirements.
    * OR one parent meets the requirements, and the other parent has a qualifying exemption benefit.
* **Qualifying exemption benefits**:
  * Carer's Allowance.
  * Contribution-based Employment and Support Allowance.
  * Incapacity Benefit.
  * Severe Disablement Allowance.
* **Parental leave exemptions**:
  * A parent can use a temporary income exemption if they are on parental leave for a different child.

## Thirty hours for working families

The system evaluates eligibility for the "thirty hours for working families" scheme.

### Summary of criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | England |
| **Age** | From 9 months to 4 years old |
| **Income limit** | Each parent must earn under £100,000 adjusted net income |

### Specific rules

* **Age limit**:
  * The child must be at least 9 months old.
  * The child must be 4 years old or younger.
* **Location requirement**:
  * The household must live in England.
* **Work and income requirements**:
  * The scheme uses the same work and income rules as the Tax-free childcare scheme.
* **Qualifying exemption benefits**:
  * Incapacity Benefit.
  * Severe Disablement Allowance.
  * Carer's Allowance.
  * Limited Capability for Work.
  * Contribution-based Employment and Support Allowance.
* **Parental leave exemptions**:
  * The scheme uses the same parental leave rules as the Tax-free childcare scheme.

## Universal credit childcare

The system evaluates eligibility for the "universal credit childcare" scheme.

### Summary of criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | Great Britain |
| **Age** | Up to 16 years old |
| **Household benefit** | Must receive Universal Credit |

### Specific rules

* **Location requirement**:
  * The household must live in Great Britain.
* **Household requirements**:
  * The household must have access to public funds.
  * The household must receive Universal Credit.
* **Age limit**:
  * The child must be 16 years old or younger.
* **Work requirements**:
  * Single parents:
    * The parent must work.
    * OR the parent must be on sick leave or parental leave.
  * Couples:
    * Both parents must work or be on sick/parental leave.
    * OR one parent works (or is on sick/parental leave) and the other parent has a qualifying exemption benefit.
* **Qualifying exemption benefits**:
  * Carer's Allowance.
  * Limited Capability for Work.
  * Limited Capability for Work-Related Activity.
